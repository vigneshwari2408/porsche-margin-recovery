## LinkedIn project description (~120 words)
**Porsche 2026 Margin Recovery — Company Lens**
Porsche's reported margin rose from 5.5% to 7.8% in H1 2026. Is the recovery real? Using only Porsche's public disclosures, I built a traceable analytics pipeline to find out:
- a PostgreSQL data model;
- an audited Excel business model;
- a 6-page Power BI management dashboard.

After removing the one-offs Porsche itself discloses, the underlying margin actually fell (9.4% → 8.4%). Lower one-off charges explain more than the whole EBIT gain, and a higher price per car offset less than half of the volume decline. I then translated Porsche's FY2026 guidance into what H2 must deliver, as scenario combinations rather than forecasts. Every number is reconciled across the tools: 1,524 checks, 0 failures.
